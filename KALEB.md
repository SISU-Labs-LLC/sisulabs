## What This Is

This is the public company website for Sisu Labs LLC. It lives at sisulabs.llc. It tells the world what Sisu Labs is, what ventures it runs, and who Daniel is. Think of it like a digital business card for the whole company. There is no login, no database, no payments. It is a simple marketing site with a few pages and some animations.

## Kill-Switch

To take the website offline:

1. **Disable Vercel deployment:** Go to Vercel Dashboard > sisulabs > Settings > Pause Project.
2. That's it. No database to worry about, no payments to stop, no crons to disable.

---

## Hand-Off

Steps to sell or transfer Sisu Labs (the company site, not the ventures):

1. **Vercel:** Transfer the project in the Vercel Dashboard.
2. **Domain:** Transfer sisulabs.llc to the buyer's registrar.
3. **GitHub:** Transfer the repo `SISU-Labs-LLC/sisulabs`. Note — this is the only public repo in the org, so the buyer gets visibility into how the company presents itself.
4. **Content:** Update the venture cards in `src/components/Ventures.tsx` and the founder bio in `src/components/Founder.tsx` to reflect the new owner.

---

## Wind-Down

If Sisu Labs is shutting down entirely and you want to take this site offline:

1. **Disable Vercel deployment** (see Kill-Switch).
2. **Archive GitHub repo:** Make it private or archive it. Download a ZIP backup first.
3. **Domain:** Let sisulabs.llc expire, or redirect it somewhere else.
4. **Note:** Shutting down this site does NOT affect the individual ventures (WorthMore, DegreeOS, etc.). Those each have their own hosting and their own KALEB.md documents.
